# Create Your First AI Influencer

Making your first AI influencer in Idolborn is simpler than it might sound. The key is to start by giving your persona a strong, believable visual identity. Once that is in place, you can use the same character across photos, videos, and talking content without rebuilding them from scratch each time.

This guide walks you through the quickest way to get there.

## 1. Create your account and enter the Studio

Start by creating an Idolborn account. You can sign up with your email and password, or continue with Google. Once you are signed in, open the **Studio**.

You can explore the workspace in preview mode, but you will need an active subscription to generate content. Before every generation, Idolborn shows the estimated token cost, so you can see what you are about to spend.

## 2. Create your persona

In **Personas**, create a new persona and give her a name and a short description.

Think of this persona as the home for everything you create for your influencer: her references, photos, videos, voice, and avatar clips.

## 3. Build her face reference

Open your persona, then go to **References** → **Face**. This is the most important step, because the face reference becomes the visual anchor for your influencer.

For a quick and original identity, choose **Blend faces**. Find two face images on Pinterest or Instagram that fit the look you have in mind, upload them, and let Idolborn blend them into one new person. Combining two inspirations usually produces a more distinctive result than relying on a single face.

Only use images you have permission to use as references, and make sure the persona you create is an adult. Idolborn asks you to confirm both points during reference creation.

Generate a few options if you can, then choose the one that feels most natural, memorable, and aligned with your idea. Mark your favourite as the default reference so it is easy to reuse later.

## 4. Create a chest-up reference

Next, go to **References** → **Chest-up**. Generate a chest-up portrait using your new face reference.

This reference shows the face, shoulders, and upper body together. It gives Idolborn more context for your influencer's proportions, styling, and overall presence, which helps later images look more consistent.

Choose a clean, well-lit portrait where the face is clearly visible. Once you have a result you like, set it as the default chest-up reference.

That is it — your first AI influencer is ready to create with.

## What each creation tab does

### Images

Use **Images** to create photos of your influencer. Describe the outfit, location, pose, mood, and camera framing you want, then select her saved references. This is where you can build a feed: mirror selfies, street-style shots, holiday photos, campaign visuals, or polished portraits.

### Video

Use **Video** to turn an image into a short clip or create a video from a prompt. You can guide the action, choose the format and duration, and, depending on the option, add a starting image, ending image, sound, or a negative prompt for details you want to avoid.

### Motion Control

Use **Motion Control** when you have a video whose movement you love. Upload a persona image and a driving video, and Idolborn transfers that movement to your influencer. It is great for recreating a gesture, walk, pose sequence, or short performance while keeping your own persona in the result.

### Avatar

The **Talking Avatar** tab makes your influencer speak on camera. Choose a portrait, add a saved speech clip or your own audio, and generate a talking video. It is a useful format for introductions, story-style updates, product mentions, and direct-to-camera content.

## Why Reel Maker is worth trying

**Reel Maker** is one of the fastest ways to turn an idea or trend into content for your persona. Upload a source reel, and Idolborn captures its first frame. In the **Face Swap** step, you create candidate versions of that frame with your influencer's identity; pick the strongest one, then use **Motion** to make a new reel that follows the original video's movement.

It is especially useful when you see a reel with a format, transition, or performance that fits your content direction. Instead of trying to describe every moment in a prompt, you can use the source reel as movement inspiration and make the result feel native to the trend — with your own consistent AI influencer at the centre.

As with any source material, make sure you have the right to use the video and any identity shown in it. Save the best candidates and finished reels in your library so you can revisit, compare, and build on them later.

## A simple first-week workflow

Once your references are ready, keep it easy: generate a small set of images in a few different settings, choose your favorites, then animate one or two into short videos. Try Reel Maker when you find a format that suits your persona, and use Talking Avatar when you want her to speak directly to the audience.
