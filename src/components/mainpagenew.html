01  /  The shift

Most people don't need another expensive course. They need a workflow that actually runs.

There's a folder of bookmarks. Three billing dashboards. A desktop covered in "final_v3.png". A notes app full of prompts that worked once and never again. And somewhere under all of it, the actual goal: a consistent AI creator model that produces content people pay for.

The courses teach the theory. The tools bill by the token. Nobody gives you the workspace where the model, the references, the prompts, and the outputs all live together — so you can stop researching and start shipping.

The model is the asset. Everything else is friction.

Persona Studio is that workspace. Built around one persona, not ten tools.
