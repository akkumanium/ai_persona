# Essential Tools and Setup

## Hardware Requirements

**Get a Separate Phone**: Do not use your personal device. I recommend an iPhone 11 or higher for these reasons:
- iPhones have higher "trust scores" with social media algorithms
- Avoids the "phone farm" penalty (platforms deprioritize accounts from cheap devices used in farms)

Cracked screen? Doesn't matter. Non-working camera? Irrelevant, you're just uploading images.

**Get a Local SIM Card**: Any cheap SIM will work. Use it exclusively for data, never connect to Wi-Fi. This ensures rotating IP addresses, protecting you from bans.

## Understanding Trust Score

Social platforms assign each account a hidden "trust score" based on the likelihood you're a legitimate user. Low trust scores reduce both overall reach and desirable demographics.

**Key Factors for Trust Score:**
- Consistency of login location
- Device consistency
- Posting patterns
- IP address stability

## Critical Don'ts

**Don't Use Wi-Fi**
Only use mobile data (4G/5G).

**Don't Use VPNs**
VPNs do NOT impact which audience you reach. They typically route through shared IPs that Instagram flags as suspicious.

**Don't Post from Multiple Locations**
Even posting from different countries while traveling can damage reach until returning to the home country.

## Should you use proxy?

This is a controversial point. From the data we have, 
we can conclude that proxy is definitely not the deciding factor in reaching a US audience or avoiding bans. 
Accounts located in Europe and Asia had no issues with reaching US audience.
Either way, if you decided to use proxy, make sure you have created the account using it and make sure you always use that same IP to avoid getting banned.

You can use one of these services: Dataimpulse, gridpanel, marsproxy, proxyline, bytepass, iproyal.

# What your model should look like?

## What NOT To Do

**Extreme Niches to Avoid:**
- Two-headed AI influencers
- Four-handed models
- 50% black/50% white split characters
- Models with extreme physical anomalies

**Why avoid these:**
- Many platforms prohibit them outright (including Fanvue)
- Poor conversion rates despite high views
- Short shelf life—once novelty wears off, engagement plummets
- You attract the wrong audience (curiosity seekers, not paying customers)

## What Actually Works

**Option 1: Basic Hot Girl**
- Simple, straightforward
- Works extremely well with proper branding
- Appeals to broadest audience
- Lower barrier to entry

**Option 2: Unique Hot Girl**
- Slightly more creative (just one step above basic)
- Examples: unusual hairstyles, unique makeup, distinctive accessories
- Stands out without being extreme
- Can go viral with more basic content

**Examples of Unique Elements That Work:**
- Eyebrow slits
- Distinctive hair clips
- Unique colored highlights
- Unusual accessories

# How to get 90%+ US Audience

## Why Do We Even Need a US Audience?

If you're running an OFM business, your goal isn't just to get views, it's to make money. And money comes from people who actually *spend*. You may get 10 million views but barely any subscriptions at Fanvue.

### The Simple Truth

The US, UK, Canada, and Western Europe make up just **20%** of social media users. Yet these same countries generate **over half of all OnlyFans revenue**—roughly $4.1 billion annually.

Meanwhile, countries like India despite having over a billion people spend about **85 times less per person** on OnlyFans than Americans do.

### The Bottom Line

Tier 1 audiences (US, UK, Canada, Western Europe) have:
- Higher disposable income
- Greater willingness to spend
- Better average lifetime value (LTV)

Tier 3 audiences (Latin America, India, North Africa, etc.) have:
- Much lower purchasing power
- Lower conversion rates
- Higher operational costs per dollar earned

**So why chase a US audience?** 

Because one US fan is worth roughly **85 Indian fans**.

The math is simple: **better audience = better business.**

## Tactical Solutions: Content Strategies for Tier 1 Audiences

### Understanding Computer Vision

Meta uses computer vision AI to analyze every piece of content frame by frame, identifying:
- Objects
- Text
- Scenery
- Audio elements

This technology categorizes content and determines which audiences receive it. All tactics below work by manipulating computer vision to serve content to your desired audience.

---

### Bucket 1: Sophistication

Create content that requires a certain level of intelligence to understand and appreciate. The best way to prevent third-world audiences from watching is to create content they don't understand.

#### Do This:

**Use English Language Content**
Non-native English speakers are less likely to engage with content requiring language comprehension.

You can combine the visual appeal of your model with jokes in English. Use suggestive hints, which only English speakers can understand.

This content:
- Requires English fluency to understand
- Appeals to Tier 1 audiences
- Shows minimal engagement from non-English speakers

**Add Captions to Every Video**
Computer vision recognizes English text and serves content to English-speaking audiences.

#### Avoid:

**Hypersexual Content**
This is the single largest cause of negative demographic drift. Content that requires no language comprehension appeals to everyone—including 80% of users you don't want.

**Thirst Traps, Flash Trends and Plain Dances**
While they generate short-term spikes, they:
- Risk bans and suspensions
- Attract overwhelmingly low-value audiences
- Damage long-term LTV

#### Mindset Shift:

**Quality Over Quantity**
A video with 12,000 views from 100% Tier 1 audiences equals the value of 1 million Indian views. Don't fear reduced reach—fear poor demographics.

---

### Bucket 2: Signs, Symbols, and Shared Cultural Vernacular

Computer vision recognizes more than text—it identifies symbols, logos, and cultural markers.

#### Strategies for Geographic Targeting

- Film in American-looking locations: You can just include "American" when you write out the location in your image and video prompts.
- Use geotagging
- Include local landmarks

---

### Bucket 3: Audio

Music and sound choices significantly impact who you reach.

#### Avoid:

**Using Trending Audio Without Thought**
If a Bollywood song is trending, understand where its viewers come from. Using it repeatedly will attract that demographic.
Another example: Using Bad Bunny will attract Latin American viewers (unless you intend to do that).

---

## Conclusion: Key Principles for Success

1. **Prioritize Audience Quality Over Quantity**
   A small Tier 1 audience outperforms a massive Tier 3 audience.

2. **Think 85x**
   One US follower equals 85 Indian followers in purchasing power.

3. **Create Barriers to Entry**
   Make content that requires speaking English to understand and appreciate.

4. **Manipulate Computer Vision Through:**
   - Language and text
   - Regional symbols and products
   - Strategic audio selection

5. **Don't Fear Reach Reduction**
   The right audience matters more than a large audience.
